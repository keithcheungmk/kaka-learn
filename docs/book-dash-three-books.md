# Three Book Dash stories

The three English stories reuse illustrations and text under CC BY 4.0, with synthesized Edge TTS narration, added word timing, and an interactive reader. They have not been human audio reviewed.

- [A House for Mouse](https://bookdash.org/books/a-house-for-mouse/), written by Michele Fry, illustrated and designed by Amy Uzzell and Jennifer Jacobs. PDF source: https://freekidsbooks.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/A-House-for-a-Mouse-bookdash.pdf
- [A Beautiful Day](https://bookdash.org/books/a-beautiful-day/), written by Elana Bregin, illustrated by Lindy Pelzl, designed by Raeesah Vawda. PDF source: https://freekidsbooks.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/a-beautiful-day_pdf-Bookdash-FKB-stories.pdf
- [A Tiny Seed](https://bookdash.org/books/a-tiny-seed/), written by Nicola Rijsdijk, illustrated by Maya Marshak, designed by Maya Marshak and Tarryn-Anne Anderson. PDF source: https://www.freekidsbooks.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/A-Tiny-Seed-The-Story-of-Wangari-Maathai.pdf

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The TTS event for “in 2011” groups those two printed words, so their shared start/end timestamps describe a phrase, not independent word boundaries. Each source's visual reading order and coordinates require review on the actual page.
